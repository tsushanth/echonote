ClearVoice Recorder - Voice-First Notes for Dyslexia, ADHD, and Anyone Who Talks Faster Than They Type

Typing is hard for some brains. ClearVoice Recorder captures your thoughts as audio AND text — record a voice note, get a transcript, both saved together. No keyboard required.

Designed For:
• Writers with dyslexia who think faster than they type
• Users with ADHD who lose ideas while spelling them out
• People with hand or wrist injuries who can't type comfortably
• Journalists capturing interviews and quotes
• Students recording lectures and study notes
• Anyone who prefers voice over typing

Voice-First Features
• High-quality audio capture: M4A, WAV, lossless options
• On-device transcription — works without internet
• Smart organization with auto-generated titles
• Search across recordings by transcript content
• Export audio or text in multiple formats

Accessibility-First Workflow
• No typing required to create or organize notes
• Voice search through your entire recording library
• Adjustable text size and high-contrast settings
• Works with screen readers for blind and low-vision users
• Hands-free playback controls

Recording Quality
• Mono and stereo recording
• Low, medium, high, and maximum quality presets
• Real-time monitoring during recording
• Auto-pause on silence (optional)

Organization
• Categories and tags for grouping recordings
• Folders for projects, classes, or topics
• Favorites for quick access

Languages, Listen, Find and Ask
• Transcribe in 41 languages. Each language downloads once and runs on your phone; a few, like Arabic, are marked Experimental
• Listen: hear key points, a paragraph or a whole transcript read aloud by a voice installed on your phone, with speed control
• Find: search every transcript by typing or voice and jump to the exact moment in the audio (voice search needs Android 13 or newer)
• Ask: see the passages most likely to answer your question, quoted from your transcripts (it finds passages, it does not write an answer)

Use Cases
• Voice journal entries you can search later
• Quick capture of ideas while walking or driving
• Interview recordings with searchable transcripts
• Lecture and meeting notes
• Audio notes for music, sermons, podcasts

How It Works:
1. Tap to record — speak your thought
2. ClearVoice transcribes automatically
3. Search, organize, and find anything by spoken word

Your voice is faster than your fingers. ClearVoice makes that an advantage.
