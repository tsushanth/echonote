New: Listen, Find and Ask. Listen reads key points, a paragraph or the whole transcript aloud with a voice on your phone, with speed control and headset or notification controls. Find searches every transcript by typing or voice and jumps to the exact moment in the audio. Ask shows the passages most likely to answer your question, quoted from your transcripts. It all runs on your phone: your audio and text never leave it. Voice search needs Android 13 or newer.
